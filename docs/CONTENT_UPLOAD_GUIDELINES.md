# Content Upload Guidelines (Admin Panel)

Welcome to the content upload guide! This document will help you understand exactly how to fill out the fields in the Admin Panel when creating a new **Blog Post** or **Case Study**. 

Following these guidelines ensures that the content looks beautiful, organized, and perfectly formatted on the front end of the website.

---

## 1. Uploading a Blog Post

When you create a new Blog, you will see several fields. Here is exactly what each field means and what you should put in it.

### **Blog Fields & Image Sizes**

| Field Name | Description | Example (Dummy Data) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Title** | The main heading of the blog. Keep it catchy and under 60 characters for best SEO. | `The Future of E-Learning in 2024` |
| **Slug** | The URL ending for the blog. Use lowercase letters and hyphens instead of spaces. | `future-of-elearning-2024` |
| **Category** | The topic this blog belongs to (e.g., EdTech, Development, Design). | `EdTech` |
| **Author** | The name of the person writing the blog. | `Jane Doe` |
| **Publish Date** | The date the blog is visible to users. | `2024-05-12` |
| **Excerpt / Short Summary** | A brief 1-2 sentence summary of what the blog is about. This appears on the blog listing page. | `Discover how AI and interactive modules are reshaping the way we learn online.` |
| **Thumbnail Image** | The main image displayed on the blog list and at the top of the article. <br>**Size:** `1200 x 630 pixels` <br>**Format:** `WebP` or `JPG` (under 200KB) | *Upload an image of a student using a laptop.* |
| **Content / Body** | The main text of the blog. Use headings (H2, H3), bullet points, and short paragraphs to make it readable. | *(See Dummy Content below)* |
| **Tags** | Keywords related to the post (helps in searching). | `E-Learning, AI, Education` |

### **Blog Dummy Content (For the 'Content' Field)**
Copy and paste this structure to see how it looks:

```markdown
## Introduction
Online learning has evolved rapidly. Today, it's not just about watching videos; it's about interactive and personalized experiences.

## The Role of AI
Artificial Intelligence is changing the game. It allows platforms to:
- Adapt to the student's learning pace.
- Provide instant feedback.
- Recommend personalized courses.

> "AI is the new UI in education." - John Smith

## Conclusion
The future is bright for e-learning. By embracing these technologies, we can make education accessible to everyone.
```

## 2. Uploading a Case Study

Case studies are more structured than blogs. They follow a problem-solution-results format.

### **Case Study Fields & Image Sizes**

| Field Name | Description | Example (Dummy Data) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Project Title** | The name of the client or the project you worked on. | `Revamping EdTech for Global Reach: ABC University` |
| **Client Name** | The name of the company or organization. | `ABC University` |
| **Industry** | The sector the client operates in. | `Higher Education` |
| **Cover Image** | The hero image for the case study. <br>**Size:** `1440 x 720 pixels` <br>**Format:** `WebP` or `JPG` (under 300KB) | *Upload a high-quality banner of a university campus.* |
| **Logo** | The client's logo (preferably with a transparent background). <br>**Size:** `400 x 400 pixels` (Square) <br>**Format:** `PNG` or `SVG` | *Upload ABC University Logo.* |
| **The Challenge (Problem)** | Describe the issue the client was facing before they came to us. | `ABC University struggled with low student engagement and a high dropout rate in their online courses.` |
| **The Solution** | How our team solved the problem. What services or products did we provide? | `We developed a custom interactive LMS with gamified learning modules and real-time analytics.` |
| **The Results (Impact)** | Measurable outcomes of our solution (use stats and numbers). | `Student engagement increased by 45%, and the dropout rate fell by 30% within the first 6 months.` |
| **Testimonial Quote** | A quote from the client praising the work. | `"The new platform completely transformed how our students learn!"` |
| **Testimonial Author** | The name and title of the person who gave the quote. | `Dr. Sarah Jenkins, Dean of E-Learning` |

### **Case Study Dummy Content Structure**
To make the case study look perfectly organized on the front end, fill out the sections like this:

**Challenge Field:**
"Before partnering with us, ABC University relied on an outdated platform that frustrated users. Students found the interface confusing, leading to a 40% drop-off rate in the first week of classes."

**Solution Field:**
"Our team conducted extensive user research and redesigned the entire platform from the ground up. We implemented:
1. A clean, intuitive dashboard.
2. Gamified achievement badges.
3. A mobile-first design for on-the-go learning."

**Results Field:**
"The impact was immediate. 
- **45% increase** in daily active users.
- **30% reduction** in course dropouts.
- Voted 'Best New LMS 2023' by EdTech Magazine."

## 💡 Pro-Tips for the Best Frontend Results

1. **Keep text concise:** Huge walls of text look bad on mobile devices. Break text into smaller paragraphs (2-3 sentences).
2. **Use lists:** Bulleted and numbered lists are much easier to read than paragraphs.
3. **Respect Image Dimensions:** Uploading a square image when a landscape banner is required (e.g., 1200x630) will result in stretched or cropped images. Always resize images *before* uploading.
4. **Compress Images:** Use tools like [TinyPNG](https://tinypng.com/) to compress images so the website loads lightning fast.
